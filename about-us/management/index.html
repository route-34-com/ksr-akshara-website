The people behind Akshara.

Founded under the KSR Educational and Charitable Trust, established in 1984.

Founder Chairman

Lion Thievathiru Dr. K.S. Rangasamy MJF

Known as KSR, he was a prominent educationist, philanthropist and visionary leader who made it his life's mission to make quality education accessible to children and young adults across the country and abroad.

He placed Tiruchengode on the global map through innovative ventures in education. Under his leadership, 16 institutions now provide education from Pre-KG to Ph.D. KSR Akshara Academy is the 15th.

Director, KSR Schools

Ms. Amutha Asaithambi

A highly respected education leader in Tamil Nadu, she leads the three KSR schools in Tiruchengode: KSR Akshara Academy (CBSE), K.S.R. Matriculation Hr. Sec. School and Avvai KSR Matriculation School.

Under her leadership Akshara has become one of the top CBSE schools in Tiruchengode, introducing cutting-edge technology and a focus on experiential learning.

She is committed to equity and accessibility in education, so that every student, regardless of background or ability, can reach their full potential.

Principal

Ms. Vijayalakshmi R.

A highly qualified educationist with a Master's degree in Education and extensive experience in teaching and administration.

Under her leadership Akshara has introduced innovative teaching methods and a wide range of educational programmes, ensuring students receive a holistic education. She is a role model for students and highly respected by Akshara's educators.

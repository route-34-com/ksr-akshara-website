Why Akshara?

Akshara has mastered the art of imparting quality, international-standard education in the Indian context, in an atmosphere that lets every learner reach their full potential.

Akshara at a glance

Scientifically researched and planned curriculum

Active, skill-based, experiential and blended learning

Unmatched safety with 24 x 7 CCTV security

Highly well-trained educators

GPS tracking on every school vehicle

Weekly and regular boarding

Dynamic sports and performing arts

Importance to scholastic and co-scholastic growth

Vision and mission

To nurture and excel in all fields, fostering our children as lifelong learners with values and leadership qualities, contributing to the global society.

Nurturing young minds and hearts of tomorrow's leaders.

Visit the campus before you decide.

Walk the classrooms, labs, library and turf with our admissions team. Admissions open every year from Vijayadasami to March.

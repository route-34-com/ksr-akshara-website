Curriculum and methodology.

The curriculum complies with CBSE expectations and builds skills for an international context. As per the New Education Policy, Akshara's classes are grouped into four stages.

FoundationPreparatoryMiddleSecondary
Pre-KG to Grade II

Foundation stage

A holistic, play-based, discovery-based programme that develops every aspect of the child: cognitive, social, emotional, creative and physical. Following NEP ECCE guidance, it prepares children for reading, writing, arithmetic and EVS in a classroom rich in sensory experiences.

The Multi-Domain system focuses on eight domains of development:

SensoryFine motorLanguageSocial skillsEmotionalSelf helpCognitionPhysical and gross motor
Grades III to V

Preparatory stage

Structured learning experiences support scholastic, interpersonal, cultural and social development. The interdisciplinary, technology-rich curriculum gives students the tools to apply what they learn, and planned skill-building activities help every learner grasp each concept.

Grades VI to VIII

Middle stage

An engaging, challenging environment that builds confidence, global understanding and civic responsibility. Teaching is project-based and hands-on, connected to real-world situations.

  • Group discussions, seminars and quizzes
  • E-research and presentations
  • Integrated IIT and NEET foundation begins
Grades IX to XII

Secondary stage

Preparing students for the most selective colleges and universities in India and abroad. A multidisciplinary system offers a range of subject combinations, with a skill enhancement programme and career guidance to help learners make confident choices.

Admissions.

Admissions are open for all classes from Vijayadasami to March for the following academic year.

Eligibility by age

Pre-KG2 years 5 months as of June
Junior KG3 years 2 months
Senior KG4 years 2 months
Grade I5 years 2 months

How to apply

  1. Enquire

    Call us or visit in person and fill in an enquiry form. You can meet the Principal or Vice Principals.

  2. Collect the application form

    Available at the school office on payment.

  3. Entrance examination

    For Senior KG onwards. Submit the completed form on the day of the exam.

  4. Confirmation

    Seats are offered first come, first served. If you miss the window, April admissions depend on availability.

Sports at Akshara.

An after-school programme that lets students explore and excel in sport, with a timetable that protects study time.

World-class football turf.

An international-standard artificial turf built for drills, full matches and evening training under floodlights.

  • Precise international dimensions
  • Durable, eco-friendly artificial grass
  • Advanced drainage
  • Floodlights for evening sessions
  • Perimeter fencing and goal netting
  • Changing rooms and showers
  • Daily practice with expert coaches
  • Specialist goalkeeper and striker coaching

How a session runs

  1. Warm-up and fitness

    15 minutes of stretching, jogging and basic exercises.

  2. Skill development

    Drills and practice with personal attention from coaches.

  3. Team play

    Teamwork, leadership and sportsmanship.

  4. Cool-down and reflection

    Students review their game and set goals.

Eight ways to play

Students compete in inter-school, district and state-level tournaments, plus friendly matches with neighbouring schools.

Visit the campus before you decide.

Walk the classrooms, labs, library and turf with our admissions team. Admissions open every year from Vijayadasami to March.
